This mod reuses textures from Create. To see them in Blockbench:

1. Open your Gradle "External Libraries" in your IDE.

2. Locate `create-(version).jar`.

3. Extract `assets/create/textures/` to a local folder (e.g., `C:/MC_Assets/Create/`).

4. In Blockbench: `File > Settings > Locations > Texture Folders.`

5. Add your local folder.

DO NOT commit Create's PNGs to this repository. (or else i get sued lol)
